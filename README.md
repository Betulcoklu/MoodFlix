# 🎬 MoodFlix

**Discover movies that match your mood and occasion.**  
MoodFlix is a mood-based movie recommendation platform that helps users quickly find films tailored to how they feel or what they’re up for — making movie nights easier, smarter, and frustration-free.

## About the Project

Choosing a movie shouldn't be overwhelming. MoodFlix solves **decision fatigue** by offering smart, mood-centered movie recommendations. MoodFlix gives you curated suggestions personalized for your current prefereces.


---

## Key Features

- 🎭 **Mood-Based Recommendations**  
  Tailored movie lists based on how you feel.

- ❤️ **Personal Watchlist**  
  Save your favorite movies for later.

- 💬 **User Interaction**  
  Rate, comment, and share your opinions.

- 🌐 **Community Recommendations**  
  Discover movies shared by other users.

- 📊 **Easy Sorting**  
  Filter films by country, release year, or genre.

---

##  Problem & Solution

>  **Problem**  
Modern streaming platforms overload users with options, leading to decision fatigue and wasted time.

>  **Solution**  
MoodFlix simplifies this by offering curated, mood-aware recommendations that match the user’s current emotional state or occasion.

---

##  Target Audience

MoodFlix is built for:
- Young adults looking for personalized entertainment
- Social groups planning spontaneous movie nights
- Movie lovers who want a smarter way to discover films
